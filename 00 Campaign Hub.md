## Where things stand
- **Sessions played:** about 15, in six arcs (see [[Session Log]]).
- **Current arc:** [[Arc 6 - Frozen Serenthia]].
- **Last scene:** the party has just come up from frozen [[Serenthia]] onto the ice sheet. [[William (Charlie)|Charlie]] is gone, taken by [[Rael]], who asked over the walkie talkie to meet at a waystation three hours northwest at midday tomorrow.
- **Next session:** the road north, the unseelie outpost and the volcano (script being written).

## What the party decided
The party chose not to go to [[Rael]]'s meeting and told him over the walkie talkie to take care of Charlie. The volcano is about to erupt, so they are heading straight for the unseelie resupply outpost two days north to learn what they can about the volcano, then rushing on to the volcano itself. They know Scott's father is alive and searching for Scott in the [[Arid Expanse]]. The next session is being planned in the "Plan outpost and volcano arc" thread.

> Rael has Charlie in his care with the party's blessing. He won't call them again this session.

## Running this arc
> [!secret] DM only
> - Information through action, not exposition. Important things are found, not told.
> - Two urgencies always run: the portal war is the clock, the personal threads are the heart.
> - [[Rael]] is not a villain.
> - Aluinius's reality-warping must become undeniable.
> - Jornt's fungi are now the party's most important tactical skill.
> - Angry GM: players win by making decisions with the information the world gives them. Never gate critical information behind a die roll. Never tell them what to do.
