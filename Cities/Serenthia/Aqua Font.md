# Aqua Font
The magical healing spring at the heart of [[Serenthia]], and an unseelie portal (see [[Fey Portals]]). Its waters invigorate mana, which is why the elves settled here.

- For thousands of years the ruling elves controlled its flow to keep the unseelie fey out. Water mages served it; [[Nergal]] was head water mage.
- At the fertility festival the ruling heir dances to gain power over it, when [[Cliodhna]] is at her most powerful.
- [[Aluinius]] jumped in at the festival and it burst open, flooding and freezing the city ([[The Drowning]]).
- Now held by [[Aelarion Frostbound]].
