# Droughtfall
The outermost ring of [[Serenthia]]. Water is rationed, the economy is farming, it is home to the lowest class, and it is under heavy military surveillance.

- The campaign began here. [[Xaphi]]'s tavern, where [[Scott]] worked and [[Aluinius]] drank, was here.
- [[Jornt]] moved here to study its fungi and mold. [[Helena]] was raised here by the midwife.
- [[The Droughtfall Movement]] fights for its people.
- Now frozen with the rest of the city.
