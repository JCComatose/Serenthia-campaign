# Elysian Oasis
The innermost ring of [[Serenthia]] (per the campaign bible). Completely flooded by the [[Aqua Font]]; the four ruling houses live on private islands in its water, the [[Elysian Sea]]. Said to be a portal to the fey realm.

- The castle is here: the [[House Torentia]] armory, [[The False Heir]] in ice, Scott's father's forge and journal.
- Site of the fertility festival and [[The Drowning]].
- [[Bixi]] once floated in its waters.
