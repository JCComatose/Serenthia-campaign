# Elysian Sea
The water inside the [[Elysian Oasis]], fed by the [[Aqua Font]], where the ruling houses had their private islands. Not the same as the [[New Ocean of Namaris]].

- [[Nergal]] and [[Bixi]] lived on it before the flood.
- Rumor: a page torn from [[Aven Quicktide]]'s _Namaris Tide Atlas, Vol. III_ shows a route to "the Eye Below", a whirlpool at the heart of the Elysian Sea.
- [[Luro the Shellwright]]'s _Shell of Recollection_ can reveal hidden fey influences or portals near its waters.
