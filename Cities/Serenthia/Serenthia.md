# Serenthia
A city built by traveling elves around the [[Aqua Font]], the only safe haven in the [[Arid Expanse]] until [[The Drowning]]. Where Serenthia had law and luxury, [[Tajir]] has survival and silver.

**Now:** completely frozen over after the Aqua Font burst. The basin it sat in is flooded, and the frozen ruins are occupied by [[Aelarion Frostbound]]'s [[Unseelie Hunt]]. The party is on the ice sheet above the city.

## The three rings
Three concentric walled rings. Movement between them is tightly controlled.

| Ring | Name | Character |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Outermost | [[Droughtfall]] | Water rationed; farming; lowest class; heavy military surveillance |
| Middle | [[Submerged ring\|Submerged Ring]] | Venice-like water levels; commerce, skilled artisans, organized crime |
| Innermost | [[Elysian Oasis]] | Flooded by the Aqua Font; the four ruling houses on private islands |

The campaign began with all the players in Droughtfall.

## Noble houses
One house rules over all of Serenthia at a time.
- [[House Aquadrion]]: ruling house
- [[House Neridia]]: commerce
- [[House Torentia]]: military
- [[House Celestia]]: the arts
