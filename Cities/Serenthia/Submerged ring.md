# Submerged Ring
The middle ring of [[Serenthia]]. Venice-like water levels; commerce, skilled artisans and organized crime. Policed by the [[Aqua Wardens]].

- **The bathhouse:** where mercenary deals are made, and home of [[Chet]]. The party found [[Helena]] here.
- **[[The Abandoned Church]]:** run by a cult; its fairy circle took the party to the fey realm.
- [[Jornt]]'s family is from here. The party holds lifetime visas to travel it freely.
- Now frozen with the rest of the city.
