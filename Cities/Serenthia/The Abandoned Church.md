# The Abandoned Church
An abandoned church in [[Serenthia]] run by a cult. A fairy circle of mushrooms inside took the party to [[The Fey Asylum]] ([[Arc 2 - The Music Box]]). Afterwards the researcher who stole the [[Music Boxes|music box]] befriended the cult.

- The fairy circle was one of the mycelium nodes [[Xaphi]] can reach [[Jornt]] through. It is now inaccessible.
