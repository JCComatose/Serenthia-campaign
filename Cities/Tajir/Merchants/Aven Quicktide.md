- **Trade:** Maps, charts, and navigation tools for both desert and sea.
- **Description:** A young cartographer with a prosthetic leg and a reputation for forging maps to lost ruins — though some turn out true.
- 
stall:
- A patched tent of white sailcloth anchored by ship masts.
- Shelves are improvised from driftwood, and his prosthetic leg squeaks when he moves across the sand.
- Dozens of rolled scrolls tied with blue cord hang from the ceiling like windchimes.
- Behind his counter sits a lockbox containing his rarest maps (secured by _Arcane Lock_).
- A small sign reads:
    > “No refunds. No promises. The map is only as honest as the man who reads it.”

Wares:
### 1. **Map of the Sunken Serpent**

- **Type:** Wondrous Item (uncommon)
    
- **Price:** 250 gp
    
- **Description:** A rolled vellum map depicting the underwater ruins of Serenthia’s trade district.
    
- **Effect:** When held over open water, the map reveals glowing ink marking the nearest known wreck within 2 miles.
    
- **Use:** Advantage on Investigation checks to locate salvageable ruins or sunken structures.
    

---

### 2. **Compass of the Wandering Star**

- **Type:** Wondrous Item (rare, requires attunement)
    
- **Price:** 800 gp
    
- **Description:** A bronze compass with a sapphire pivot that glows when you’re near a leyline or magic current.
    
- **Effect:** Grants +2 to Survival (navigation) checks and always points toward the nearest magical convergence.
    
- **Flaw:** The compass spins uncontrollably in areas corrupted by Fey energy, imposing disadvantage on Wisdom (Survival) for 1 minute.
    

---

### 3. **Desertwalker’s Chart**

- **Type:** Consumable Map (uncommon)
    
- **Price:** 120 gp
    
- **Description:** A thin scroll of treated silk that shifts with the dunes.
    
- **Effect:** When unrolled, creates a minor _illusory_ topographic projection of the desert within a 1-mile radius, accurate to recent terrain shifts. Lasts 10 minutes.
    

---

### 4. **Namaris Tide Atlas, Vol. III**

- **Type:** Book (rare)
    
- **Price:** 500 gp
    
- **Description:** A heavy tome with embossed leather, filled with tide patterns, moon cycles, and Fey current anomalies.
    
- **Effect:** If studied for 1 hour before travel, grants advantage on any check to navigate flooded or magical waters for the next 24 hours.
    
- **Hidden Note:** One page is torn out — supposedly showing a route to “the Eye Below,” a whirlpool at the heart of the Elysian Sea.
    

---

### 5. **Stormglass Navigator**

- **Type:** Wondrous Item (uncommon)
    
- **Price:** 350 gp
    
- **Description:** A crystalline vial with swirling liquid that reacts to weather and magical flux.
    
- **Effect:** You can predict weather and planar disturbances within 24 hours. Once per day, you can cast _Detect Magic_ centered on yourself.
    

---

### 6. **Map of Shifting Sands**

- **Type:** Wondrous Item (rare)
    
- **Price:** 700 gp
    
- **Description:** A parchment map whose ink writhes as you move it.
    
- **Effect:** When you spend 10 minutes studying it, you can magically sense all major moving dunes, caravans, or storm fronts within 5 miles for 1 hour.
    

---

### 7. **Tidecaller’s Sextant**

- **Type:** Wondrous Item (rare, requires attunement)
    
- **Price:** 1,000 gp
    
- **Description:** A gilded sextant with small Fey runes. Its mirrors shimmer with reflections of a different sea.
    
- **Effect:** When used to navigate, you can travel twice as fast over water without losing your bearings. Once per day, you can cast _Water Breathing_ (self only).
    
- **Secret:** If used during a full moon, you briefly glimpse a drowned city beneath the waves — which moves location each month.
    

---

### 8. **The Ink of Endless Currents**

- **Type:** Wondrous Item (uncommon)
    
- **Price:** 200 gp per vial
    
- **Description:** Blue-black ink that shifts like tides. Aven claims it’s distilled from Leviathan’s blood.
    
- **Effect:** Any map drawn with this ink glows faintly in starlight, revealing the safest path across desert or sea.
    
- **Mechanic:** If used to draw or mark, grants +5 to navigation-related skill checks for 24 hours.
    

---

### 9. **Forged Map to the Vault of Avariel**

- **Type:** Plot Hook Item
    
- **Price:** 150 gp
    
- **Description:** A map to a supposed vault of winged elves buried under the basin cliffs.
    
- **Truth:** It’s partially real — it leads to a ruin, but one infested with Fey-corrupted harpies.
    
- **Use:** Serves as a perfect side quest for exploration or treasure hunting.
    

---

### 10. **Aven’s Tide Compass (Personal)**

- **Type:** Unique Artifact (can be stolen or bartered for)
    
- **Price:** Not for sale (or 5,000 gp)
    
- **Description:** Aven’s prosthetic leg doubles as a retractable spyglass and compass combined.
    
- **Effect:**
    
    - Grants +3 to Navigation (Survival) checks while carried.
        
    - Allows user to cast _Augury_ once per day when consulting it over a map or chart.
        
    - When submerged in water, faint whispers describe “safe passage” — though only Aven seems able to interpret them.
