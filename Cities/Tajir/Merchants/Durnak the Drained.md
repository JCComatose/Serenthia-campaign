- **Trade:** Water, fey-distilled liquors, and preservation salts.
- **Description:** A gray-skinned dwarf who once worked the pumps of Serenthia before the flood. Now he bottles “memory-water” — collected from drowned wells, said to retain echoes of voices and scenes.
Stall:
- A curved counter made of sun-bleached wood, stained blue by years of spills.
- Dozens of bottles hang from the canopy above, clinking softly.
- A rusted pump mechanism trickles water into a basin that he uses to “test purity.”
- The air smells of salt, mold, and strong spirits.
- Hidden under the stall is a lockbox containing his rarer goods (protected by _Alarm_ and a mechanical spring trap).
Wares:
### 1. **Memory-Water (Common to Rare)**

- **Price:** 50–250 gp per vial (depending on strength)
    
- **Description:** Bottled from the drowned wells of Serenthia, the liquid shows faint shimmering images when shaken.
    
- **Effect:** Drinking it grants the drinker fleeting visions of the past — moments of joy, fear, or love.
    
    - **Mechanic:** Once per day, a drinker can gain advantage on an Investigation or History check related to Serenthia.
        
    - **Side Effect:** A 5% chance of reliving a memory too vividly — causing 1 level of exhaustion.
        

---

### 2. **Feywine of the Drowned Moon**

- **Type:** Wondrous Consumable (uncommon)
    
- **Price:** 200 gp per bottle
    
- **Description:** Pale blue wine that glows under moonlight. Said to be distilled from dew gathered in the Feywild’s endless winter.
    
- **Effect:** When consumed, grants advantage on Charisma (Persuasion) checks for 1 hour.
    
    - If drunk under moonlight, also grants 1 use of _Charm Person_ (DC 13).
        
    - Side Effect: The drinker’s eyes glow faintly silver for 24 hours.
        

---

### 3. **Salt of the Still Current**

- **Type:** Alchemical Ingredient / Combat Item
    
- **Price:** 80 gp per pouch
    
- **Description:** Fine crystalline salt gathered from evaporated Fey pools.
    
- **Effect:** When scattered on the ground, creates a 10 ft. radius circle where water magic is suppressed for 1 minute (as _Dispel Magic_ vs. DC 15 for water-based spells).
    
- **Use:** Favored by smugglers and spell-duelists.
    

---

### 4. **Tears of the Pumpmaster**

- **Type:** Potion (rare)
    
- **Price:** 500 gp
    
- **Description:** A cloudy blue liquid in a metal flask, said to be Durnak’s own tears — though he swears it’s seawater.
    
- **Effect:** Acts as a **Potion of Heroism**, but while under its effect, the drinker hears muffled voices calling from beneath the waves.
    

---

### 5. **Bitter Brine Ale**

- **Type:** Common Beverage
    
- **Price:** 3 gp per bottle
    
- **Description:** A foul-smelling ale that leaves a salty aftertaste and mild euphoria.
    
- **Effect:** Gives temporary resistance to cold damage for 10 minutes (once per long rest).
    
- **Side Effect:** Disadvantage on Wisdom (Insight) checks while drunk.
    

---

### 6. **Preservation Salts of Namaris**

- **Type:** Alchemical Item (uncommon)
    
- **Price:** 100 gp per pouch (3 uses)
    
- **Description:** White powder that instantly desiccates anything organic it touches.
    
- **Effect:** Used to preserve bodies, meat, or relics — stops decay for up to 10 days.
    
    - When thrown (action), can deal 2d6 necrotic damage to one creature of flesh (DC 12 Con save for half).
        

---

### 7. **Brineglass Decanter**

- **Type:** Wondrous Item (uncommon)
    
- **Price:** 350 gp
    
- **Description:** A hand-blown glass jug that refills with clean water once per day — or seawater if angered.
    
- **Effect:** Produces 1 gallon of potable water every dawn. If the user takes psychic damage, the next use produces saltwater instead.
    

---

### 8. **Durnak’s “Floodfire” Flask**

- **Type:** Alchemical Weapon (rare)
    
- **Price:** 400 gp
    
- **Description:** A sealed flask of volatile blue liquid.
    
- **Effect:** When thrown (range 20/60 ft.), it bursts in a 10 ft. radius, dealing **4d6 fire damage** and **2d6 cold damage** (Dex save DC 14 for half). The area becomes slick and lightly obscured by steam for 1 round.
    
- **Rumor:** Durnak brewed it by accident when he cried into a still.
    

---

### 9. **Echoing Gourd**

- **Type:** Wondrous Item (uncommon)
    
- **Price:** 250 gp
    
- **Description:** A dried gourd painted with runes that hum faintly. When held to the ear, it repeats distant sounds.
    
- **Effect:** Once per day, you can replay the last 10 minutes of sound captured within 60 feet of the gourd.
    
- **Use:** Favored by spies, drunks, and nostalgic widows.
    

---

### 10. **The Drowned Draught (Unique)**

- **Type:** Legendary Consumable
    
- **Price:** Not for sale (rumored 2,000 gp)
    
- **Description:** A black glass bottle sealed with red wax. Inside swirls what looks like starlight in water.
    
- **Effect (Unknown to most):** The drinker immediately experiences a vivid, prophetic dream tied to Cliodhna’s song — possibly opening a rift to the Feywild in their sleep.
    
- **Durnak’s Story:** “Don’t drink it unless you’re ready to drown in your own memories.”
