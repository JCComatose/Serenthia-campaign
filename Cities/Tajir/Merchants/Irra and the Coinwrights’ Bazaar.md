- **Trade:** Practical goods, ship repairs, tools, and supplies for expeditions.
- **Description:** A vast covered market run by **Irra Coinwright**, a hard-eyed human woman with a golden tooth and a ledger thicker than a spellbook. She taxes every deal and enforces it with hired swords.

Wares:
### 1. **Dune Skimmer Repair Kits**

- **Price:** 75 gp
    
- **Description:** Compact kit containing rope, sealant, bronze rivets, and sand-resistant bearings.
    
- **Effect:** Allows a vehicle or airship repair check without penalty; grants advantage on related checks.
    

---

### 2. **Expeditioner’s Ration Crate**

- **Price:** 40 gp (feeds 4 for 10 days)
    
- **Contents:** Salted meat, preserved cactus fruit, and iron-brewed tea blocks.
    
- **Effect:** Reduces risk of disease or food spoilage in extreme heat.
    

---

### 3. **Coinwright’s Compass** _(Uncommon Wondrous Item)_

- **Price:** 300 gp
    
- **Description:** A steel compass with golden etchings that always points toward the nearest functioning trade route or settlement.
    
- **Effect:** Once per day, you may use it to cast _Locate Object_ (on trade goods or coinage only).
    

---

### 4. **Water Purifier Lens** _(Rare Wondrous Item)_

- **Price:** 500 gp
    
- **Description:** A polished brass lens mounted in a steel frame; when sunlight passes through it, water beneath is purified.
    
- **Effect:** Purifies up to 10 gallons of water per day when exposed to sunlight for an hour.
    

---

### 5. **Sandglass of Honest Weights**

- **Type:** Utility Item (Uncommon)
    
- **Price:** 150 gp
    
- **Description:** A small, rune-etched hourglass that glows green when deals are even and red when one side is cheating.
    
- **Effect:** Functions as a magical _Zone of Truth_ for trade negotiations (DC 13 Charisma save to resist).
    

---

### 6. **Coinwright’s Contract** _(Scroll, Rare)_

- **Price:** 600 gp
    
- **Description:** Blank parchment that binds a signed agreement in arcane law.
    
- **Effect:** Any signed contract cannot be broken without both parties succeeding on a DC 18 Charisma saving throw; failure inflicts 2d6 psychic damage as guilt sears the mind.
    

---

### 7. **Hull Sealant Paste**

- **Price:** 25 gp per jar (5 uses)
    
- **Description:** A foul-smelling compound used to waterproof ship hulls or reinforce armor seams.
    
- **Effect:** Applied to wooden or leather armor, grants resistance to acid damage for 1 hour.
    

---

### 8. **Coinwright Token** _(Common Trinket)_

- **Price:** 10 gp
    
- **Description:** A stamped brass coin bearing Irra’s mark — proof of paid tithe or loyalty.
    
- **Effect:** Grants advantage on Persuasion checks when dealing with Irra’s network.
    

---

### 9. **Trade Pigeon Kit**

- **Price:** 80 gp
    
- **Description:** A messenger pigeon in a reinforced cage with a brass ring that ensures it always returns to Tajir.
    
- **Effect:** Can send up to 25 words magically back to Tajir once per day.
    

---

### 10. **Irra’s Ledger of the Lost (Unique Item)**

- **Type:** Wondrous Item (Very Rare)
    
- **Description:** A thick brass-bound ledger filled with the names of merchants who vanished after the flood.
    
- **Effect:** When a name is written in it, Irra can scry on that person once per week (no saving throw if they owe her money).
    
- **Rumor:** Some say Irra writes names of the dead just to see if they still answer.
