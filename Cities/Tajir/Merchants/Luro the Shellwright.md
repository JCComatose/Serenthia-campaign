- **Trade:** Armor, jewelry, and weaponry crafted from enormous seashells.
- **Description:** A broad-shouldered craftsman with shark-tooth tattoos. He dives into the flooded ruins below Tajir, claiming the shells are gifts from “the ocean that remembers.”
### 1. **Coralplate Breastguard** _(Uncommon Armor — counts as Scale Mail)_

- **Price:** 450 gp
    
- **Description:** Overlapping coral scales reinforced with nacre and kelp cord. Faintly bioluminescent veins run through the chest.
    
- **Stats:** AC 14 + Dex (max 2); counts as light armor for swimming and grants advantage on Athletics (swimming) checks.
    
- **Special:** While submerged, wearer gains resistance to cold damage.
    

---

### 2. **Tideglass Pendant** _(Wondrous Item, Common)_

- **Price:** 100 gp
    
- **Description:** A tear-shaped charm filled with swirling seawater that never spills.
    
- **Effect:** Wearer can hold their breath twice as long as normal. Once per day, the pendant glows to warn of nearby poison or rot (within 10 ft).
    

---

### 3. **Wavecaller Blade** _(Weapon, Rare, longsword or scimitar)_

- **Price:** 850 gp
    
- **Description:** Forged from sharpened shell and barnacle-encrusted steel. When swung, it hums like a crashing wave.
    
- **Stats:** +1 to attack and damage rolls; on a critical hit, target must succeed on a DC 13 Strength save or be pushed 10 ft.
    
- **Special:** Once per day, as a bonus action, you may summon a burst of seawater (10 ft cone, Dex save 13, 2d6 bludgeoning).
    

---

### 4. **Pearl of Stillwater** _(Uncommon Wondrous Item)_

- **Price:** 300 gp
    
- **Description:** A small pearl that radiates calm.
    
- **Effect:** As an action, the user can cast _Calm Emotions_ once per day. In addition, when submerged, they cannot be magically frightened.
    

---

### 5. **Shellgreave Boots** _(Uncommon Wondrous Item)_

- **Price:** 250 gp
    
- **Description:** Reinforced with abalone plates and shark hide.
    
- **Effect:** Wearer’s speed is not reduced by difficult terrain in water or sand. Once per long rest, you may use a bonus action to “ground” yourself, gaining +2 AC until your next turn if you haven’t moved.
    

---

### 6. **Echo Pearl Earrings** _(Rare Wondrous Item)_

- **Price:** 500 gp
    
- **Description:** Twin iridescent pearls that vibrate faintly when spoken into.
    
- **Effect:** Functions as _Sending Stones_ but only underwater or within 1 mile of a large body of water. Voices sound like they’re speaking through bubbles.
    

---

### 7. **Nacre Dagger** _(Weapon, Common)_

- **Price:** 50 gp
    
- **Description:** A small blade carved from pure nacre, light and sharp as glass.
    
- **Stats:** Dagger (1d4 piercing, finesse, light).
    
- **Special:** On hit, target takes an extra 1 acid damage if submerged or damp.
    

---

### 8. **Whispering Shell Circlet** _(Wondrous Item, Rare)_

- **Price:** 700 gp
    
- **Description:** A delicate band adorned with shell spirals. When worn, faint ocean sounds fill the ears.
    
- **Effect:** Grants the ability to comprehend and verbally communicate with aquatic beasts. Once per day, can cast _Detect Thoughts_ when near water.
    

---

### 9. **Conch Horn of Rallying** _(Uncommon Wondrous Item, Instrument)_

- **Price:** 400 gp
    
- **Description:** A massive pink conch fitted with silver bands.
    
- **Effect:** When blown as an action, allies within 30 ft gain 1d4 temporary HP and advantage on saving throws against fear for 1 minute. Can be used 1/day.
    

---

### 10. **The Shell of Recollection** _(Very Rare Wondrous Item)_

- **Price:** 1,500 gp
    
- **Description:** A massive nautilus shell inscribed with runes that seem to shimmer with faint blue light.
    
- **Effect:** Once per week, the holder may view a one-minute “memory” from the ocean — scenes from drowned Serenthia, echoes of voices lost below.
    
- **Hidden Power:** When used near the waters of the Elysian Sea, this memory can reveal _hidden Fey influences or portals_.
