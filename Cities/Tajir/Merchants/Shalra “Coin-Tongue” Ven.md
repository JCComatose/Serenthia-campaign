A half-elf with burn-scarred hands who keeps ledgers written in three languages — and sometimes blood. She claims to have contacts in every ring of the drowned city.
**Trade:** Rare relics, Serenthian salvage, and forbidden curios.
Wares:
### 1. **The Drowned Compass**

- **Type:** Wondrous Item (rare, requires attunement)
    
- **Price:** 850 gp
    
- **Description:** A rusted brass compass filled with shimmering Fey water. The needle trembles toward the nearest source of _unsealed planar energy_ — such as Fey rifts, leyline breaches, or Unseelie beacons.
    
- **Stats:** Once per long rest, you can ask the compass to “seek the breach.” For 1 hour, you gain advantage on Survival checks to navigate toward any Fey-aligned portal or disturbance.
    

---

### 2. **A Bottle of Last Breath**

- **Type:** Consumable (uncommon)
    
- **Price:** 300 gp
    
- **Description:** A stoppered vial said to contain the final breath of a noble who drowned in Serenthia.
    
- **Effect:** When opened, the user can breathe underwater for 10 minutes. However, roll a **DC 13 Wisdom saving throw** — on a failure, you hear whispers of the drowned dead for 1 hour, imposing disadvantage on Insight checks.
    

---

### 3. **The Mirror Coin**

- **Type:** Wondrous Item (uncommon)
    
- **Price:** 500 gp
    
- **Description:** A two-faced silver coin with one side showing a smiling mask and the other a blank void.
    
- **Stats:** Once per day, you can flip the coin as a bonus action. On _heads_, gain advantage on your next Charisma (Deception or Persuasion) check. On _tails_, you gain disadvantage on Wisdom saving throws until the end of your next turn.
    
- **Curse (Hidden):** If used three times in a day, your reflection whispers your true name in the next mirror you pass.
    

---

### 4. **Glassheart Pendant**

- **Type:** Wondrous Item (rare, attunement required by a spellcaster)
    
- **Price:** 1,200 gp
    
- **Description:** A cracked crystal heart on a thin chain. Within it, water swirls like smoke.
    
- **Effect:** While attuned, once per day when you cast a spell of 3rd level or lower, you can force one creature affected by it to make the save at **disadvantage**. However, the next time you fail a concentration check, you take 2d8 psychic damage.
    

---

### 5. **Scrimshawed Conch of Serenthia**

- **Type:** Wondrous Item (uncommon)
    
- **Price:** 400 gp
    
- **Description:** Carved with ancient Serenthian script — supposedly from the basin’s noble fleet.
    
- **Effect:** When held to your ear, you can hear faint music from the drowned city. Once per week, you can blow into it to cast _Sending_ (no spell slot).
    

---

### 6. **Blackwater Phial**

- **Type:** Potion (rare)
    
- **Price:** 650 gp
    
- **Description:** A potion bottle filled with dark, viscous fluid.
    
- **Effect:** Upon drinking, gain resistance to acid and poison for 1 hour. However, your veins visibly blacken — for the duration, Fey and undead creatures automatically recognize you as “touched by the drowned.”
    

---

### 7. **Ledger of Unspoken Debts**

- **Type:** Wondrous Item (rare)
    
- **Price:** 1,500 gp
    
- **Description:** A tattered book that can record promises, deals, and debts made in its presence. The writing appears in blood.
    
- **Effect:** Any deal spoken within 10 ft. of the open ledger becomes magically binding — both parties must attempt a **DC 16 Charisma saving throw** to willingly break the agreement.
    
- **Curse (Hidden):** Breaking a deal causes 3d10 psychic damage to both parties and etches their names permanently into the ledger.
    

---

### 8. **Sandglass of False Time**

- **Type:** Wondrous Item (rare)
    
- **Price:** 950 gp
    
- **Description:** A small hourglass filled with shimmering desert sand. When flipped, the sand falls upward instead of down.
    
- **Effect:** Once per long rest, you can reverse a single round (6 seconds) of combat, returning all creatures and effects to their previous positions and states. Afterwards, the user must succeed on a **DC 15 Constitution save** or gain one level of exhaustion.
    

---

### 9. **The Gilded Eye**

- **Type:** Trinket / Possible Quest Item
    
- **Price:** 200 gp
    
- **Description:** A golden prosthetic eye that seems to follow whoever speaks. It hums faintly near Fey energy.
    
- **Effect:** While carried, you can see faint outlines of hidden portals or invisible creatures as shimmering distortions (no mechanical advantage, but great for perception flavor).
    
- **Secret:** If placed into an empty socket, it allows _True Sight (10 ft)_ for 1 minute, once per day — but each use permanently lowers your max HP by 1.
    

---

### 10. **Shalra’s Blood Note**

- **Type:** Contract / Plot Hook
    
- **Price:** Negotiable
    
- **Description:** A parchment written in her own blood, signed with a shifting rune.
    
- **Effect:** The bearer may call in **one favor** from Shalra Ven — but doing so marks their hand with the same burn scars she bears.
    
- **Mechanic:** You may auto-succeed a single Persuasion or Intimidation check involving Tajir’s factions. However, Fey-aligned beings will sense the mark and react with hostility.
