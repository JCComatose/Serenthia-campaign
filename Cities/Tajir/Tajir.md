Tajir is a city built on the sun bleached cliffs that create the northern edge of [[The Great Basin]]. Tajir is the remnants of a civilization from long ago but the ruins of that once great city and its proximity to [[Serenthia]] made it a great choice for traders to establish themselves. While Serenthia had law and luxury, Tajir has survival and silver. Now on the last stretch of solid land before the [[New Ocean of Namaris]], this trading center is in the beginning phases of turning into a city. Due to its notoriety amongst traders and the knowledge of its placement to the new ocean, nomadic merchants are returning to setup permanent bases.

## Since the party arrived
- [[Nergal]] dropped the party here after [[The Drowning]] ([[Arc 5 - The Arid Expanse and Tajir]]), and they helped it grow from an outpost into a town.
- **Leader:** [[Thelin]], who thinks the party is from [[Tinglewood]].
- The [[Aqua Wardens]] and the [[Aqua Warden Chief]] are regrouping here with Serenthia's evacuees.
- **The party's home:** the phoenix egg is buried beneath it. The ground around it has turned to obsidian ([[The Obsidian Formation]]), and it trembles.
- Scott's father is walking here from Serenthia.
