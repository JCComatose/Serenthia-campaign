# Tinglewood
_The campaign bible spells it **Tingleywood**; spelling being confirmed with jesse._

A town [[Aluinius]] invented, which turned out to be real: things he makes up or misremembers tend to become real.

- The party fetched wood from it and brought back [[Tinglewoodyallen]], Aluinius's invented daughter, and her fire elemental lover ([[Arc 5 - The Arid Expanse and Tajir]]).
- Aluinius told [[Thelin]] of [[Tajir]] that the party is from here.
- See [[Tinglewood Is Real]].
