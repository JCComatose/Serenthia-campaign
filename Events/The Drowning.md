# The Drowning
The burst of the [[Aqua Font]] that flooded and froze [[Serenthia]], at the end of [[Arc 4 - Into the Elysian Oasis]].

## What happened
- At the fertility festival, [[Helena]] performed the heir's water dance as part of [[Lady Neridia]]'s plan, meant to throw [[Neridia's Gem]] into the font.
- Guards attacked. At the critical moment [[Cliodhna]] called out to [[Aluinius]]. Thinking it was [[Xaphi]], he jumped into the Aqua Font.
- The font, an unseelie portal the ruling elves had held shut for generations, burst open. Serenthia flooded entirely, then froze.

## Consequences
- Serenthia is frozen solid in a flooded basin. [[Aelarion Frostbound]]'s [[Unseelie Hunt]] holds the ruins.
- The flood formed the [[New Ocean of Namaris]].
- [[Nergal]] rescued the party on [[Bixi]] and dropped them at [[Tajir]].
- The [[Aqua Wardens]] evacuated survivors to Tajir. Scott's father survived and is walking toward Tajir.
- The fate of the noble houses is unknown; [[The False Heir]] was found frozen in the castle.
