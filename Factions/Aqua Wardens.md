# Aqua Wardens
The police force of [[Serenthia]]'s [[Submerged ring|Submerged Ring]], led by the [[Aqua Warden Chief]].

- Recruited the party in [[Arc 1 - Droughtfall and the Water Crisis]]; the reward was lifetime visas to the Submerged Ring.
- [[The Rookie]] was one of theirs, and a double agent.
- After [[The Drowning]] they evacuated Serenthia and are regrouping at [[Tajir]].
