# House Aquadrion
_The campaign bible spells it **Aquadrian**; spelling being confirmed with jesse._

The ruling house of [[Serenthia]]. Four noble houses, all descended from the elves who discovered the [[Aqua Font]], govern the city; one house rules over all of Serenthia at any given time, and that was House Aquadrion when the city fell. The others are [[House Neridia]], [[House Torentia]] and [[House Celestia]].

- Its heir performs a water dance at the fertility festival to gain power over the Aqua Font. [[The False Heir]] had been failing year after year.
- [[Helena]] is the true heir, swapped at birth.
- [[Lady Neridia]] (with [[Nergal]]) plotted to overthrow the house, and used Helena to do it.
- The false heir was found encased in ice in a locked room in frozen Serenthia.
