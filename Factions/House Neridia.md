# House Neridia
One of the four noble houses of [[Serenthia]]; its domain is commerce. Led by its matriarch, [[Lady Neridia]].

- Lady Neridia used [[Helena]] and [[Neridia's Gem]] to try to shut the [[Aqua Font]], topple [[House Aquadrion|House Aquadrian]] and seize power. The scheme ended in [[The Drowning]].
- Whether the house survived is unknown.
