# House Torentia
One of the four noble houses of [[Serenthia]]: the house of the armed forces.

- Its armory survives, frozen, inside the castle in the [[Elysian Oasis]]. The party looted it in [[Arc 6 - Frozen Serenthia]]: weapons, armor, Vials of Living Water, the [[Tide Blade]] and the Warden's Cloak.
