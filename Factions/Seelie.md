# The Seelie Court
One of the two fey courts whose war sets the world's climate. More seelie control of the [[Fey Portals]] makes the world hotter and drier. The world is desert because the seelie are winning.

- Long ago the Seelie court closed the unseelie portals on [[Namaris]] to weaken the [[Unseelie]], since each court's power is channeled through its expression on Namaris.
- The party has not met the Seelie yet. They presumably want the party's help too, or may want to use them as pawns just as the unseelie do.
