# The Unseelie Hunt
The unseelie war band commanded by [[Aelarion Frostbound]]. It holds frozen [[Serenthia]] and the burst [[Aqua Font]], and is racing to secure the remaining [[Fey Portals]].

- The Hunt pursues specific individuals. [[Aluinius]] is one.
- Includes Frost Shades: Aelarion's Herald is one, and dying Frost Shades leave ice shards with mycelium patterns in them.
- Runs a staging camp at [[Hollis's Gas Station]] for the attack on the volcano: 12 riders and 4 frost hounds under a captain (default numbers, 2026-10-12).
- [[Aelarion Frostbound]] himself is in [[The Fey Realm]], fighting on the far side of the volcano portal. If the Hunt can't convert the portal, he closes it completely: less power for the unseelie if they win, but better than leaving it to the seelie. A captain leads the assault in his place.
