# The Unseelie Court
The fey court of water and winter. More unseelie control of the [[Fey Portals]] makes the world colder and wetter.

- For thousands of years the ruling elves of [[Serenthia]] controlled the flow of the [[Aqua Font]], an unseelie portal, to keep the unseelie fey out of the world.
- When [[Aluinius]] jumped in, it burst open, flooding and freezing Serenthia and opening the portal ([[The Drowning]]).
- Now the [[Unseelie Hunt]] under [[Aelarion Frostbound]] holds the ruins and is racing to take the other portals.
