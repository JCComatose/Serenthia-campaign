# Fey Portals
The war between the [[Seelie]] and [[Unseelie]] courts decides the world's climate. Whoever controls more portals controls the climate:
- More seelie control: hotter and drier.
- More unseelie control: colder and wetter.

The current desert exists because the seelie are winning. [[Aelarion's Portal Map]] shows six portals, two crossed out.

| Portal | Location | Status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [[Aqua Font]] | Frozen [[Serenthia]] | Unseelie: burst open, Aelarion holds the site |
| Phoenix | [[Arid Expanse]] | Believed secured by the unseelie, but the egg survives under [[Tajir]] ([[The Obsidian Formation]]) |
| Volcano | Far north | Contested in [[The Station and the Volcano]]: the sun clan's ritual would turn it seelie, the Hunt wants it unseelie, and Aelarion will close it from the fey side if the Hunt can't convert it |
| Hot Spring | Eastern Expanse | Active unseelie target |
| Fire Elemental | Deep desert | Active unseelie target |
| Unknown sixth | Farther south | A black circle on Aelarion's map with no annotation in Common |

## Open questions
- **The phoenix portal:** Aelarion thinks it is secured. If the egg hatches, who controls it?
- **The sixth portal:** farther south, and it leads into [[The Fey Realm]] (jesse, 2026-10-12). What waits there is not decided yet.
- The volcano and the race for it are being planned in the "Plan outpost and volcano arc" thread.

> [!secret] DM only: the end goal
> Portals can be converted between seelie and unseelie from this side, but from inside [[The Fey Realm]] they can be closed for good, one by one. If every portal is closed, the war between the courts can no longer touch the material plane. This is where the campaign is heading. See [[Closing the Portals]].
>
> At the end of [[The Station and the Volcano]], [[Aelarion Frostbound]] plays friendly and points the party to the sixth portal. It's a trap to get them into the fey realm, planned for a later session.
