# The Fey Asylum
An abandoned insane asylum in [[The Fey Realm]], sealed by magical barriers. The party arrived here through the fairy circle in [[The Abandoned Church]] ([[Arc 3 - The Fey Asylum]]).

## Inmates
- [[Arty Ramsden]], blood magic fey painter
- [[Booker]], gnome mob boss with an invisible family
- [[The Exiled Unseelie]], in the attic

The party was sent home after refusing to help the exile. [[Xaphi]] did not come back with them.
