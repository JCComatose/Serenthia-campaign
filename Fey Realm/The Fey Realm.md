# The Fey Realm
The realm of the [[Seelie]] and [[Unseelie]] courts. Leaving it makes you begin to forget, which is why [[Aluinius]] remembers so little.

- The party visited [[The Fey Asylum]] in [[Arc 3 - The Fey Asylum]].
- [[Xaphi]] is trapped here.
- Mycelium networks and fairy circles connect it to the material plane, and they recognize [[Jornt]]. The [[Elysian Oasis]] is said to be a portal here.
