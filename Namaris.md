# Namaris
The world the Serenthia campaign takes place in. It is almost entirely desert, the [[Arid Expanse]]: a vast, hostile wilderness of roving gangs, sand raiders and desert monsters. Until the flood, the only safe haven was [[Serenthia]].

## History
Thousands of years ago Namaris was a beautiful forest world, when the powers of the [[Seelie]] and [[Unseelie]] courts were in balance. The fey keep this balance through [[Fey Portals]]: whoever holds more of them sets the climate.

The unseelie portals began to close until there was but one, the [[Aqua Font]]. This put the world into perpetual drought and turned it to desert.

Ruins of what once existed are scattered across the world. The people of those cities reverted to tribalism, and the knowledge of the old world disappeared.

A wandering tribe of elves walking through [[The Great Basin]] discovered an oasis with a spring at its center, the Aqua Font. Bathing in its waters invigorated their mana and gave them strength, so they settled, growing from a village into [[Serenthia]]. The original families vied for power and became the four noble houses ([[House Aquadrion]], [[House Neridia]], [[House Torentia]], [[House Celestia]]). For generations the ruling elves controlled the font's flow to keep the unseelie fey out of the world.

At the fertility festival, [[Aluinius]] jumped into the font. It burst open, flooding Serenthia and then freezing it ([[The Drowning]]). The unseelie portal is now open.

## Modern day
- Serenthia is frozen solid, its basin flooded, and the ruins are held by [[Aelarion Frostbound]]'s [[Unseelie Hunt]].
- The flood formed the [[New Ocean of Namaris]]. Dried riverbeds flow again and the land is turning toward woodland, coast, plains and timber mountains.
- [[Tajir]], on the new ocean's edge, is growing into a town.
- The unseelie are racing to take the remaining portals.

## The future
> [!secret] DM only
> To the Unseelie court, this world is purely a tool. If they take the remaining portals, balance is gone: the planet drowns, then winter comes and it freezes over completely. See [[Cliodhna's Endgame]].
