# Aelarion's Portal Map
Found in [[Aelarion Frostbound]]'s war room in frozen [[Serenthia]] ([[Arc 6 - Frozen Serenthia]]). It shows all six [[Fey Portals]], two of them crossed out, and a sixth location marked with a black circle and no annotation in Common.

- [[Rael]] also has Aelarion's map of the expanse.
