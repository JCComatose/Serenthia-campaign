# Dragon's Tongue
Part of the [[Herbs of Namaris]] table (d20 roll: 4).

**Use:** After being cooked for 1d4 hours, this spicy pepper deals 2d8 fire damage to the creature that eats it and gives them one free 1st-level casting of burning hands (DC 13), which is expelled out their mouth.
