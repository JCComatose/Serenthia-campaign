# Starbloom
Part of the [[Herbs of Namaris]] table (d20 roll: 11).

**Use:** When distilled under starlight for 8 hours, the liquid from this plant can be splashed over a 20-foot cube to apply the effects of the faerie fire spell for 1 minute. (DC 13) (No concentration needed.)
