# Music Boxes
- **The first box:** stolen, then recovered by the party in [[Arc 2 - The Music Box]]. A researcher had taken it to caverns; a jewel taken from an undead captain fit it. When opened, its legend song compelled [[Aluinius]] to walk to [[The Abandoned Church]], where a fairy circle took the party to the fey realm.
- **The second box:** a comatose outpost commander was found with another box of the same type.

## Open question
Someone in the unseelie operation is using these boxes. Purpose unknown.
