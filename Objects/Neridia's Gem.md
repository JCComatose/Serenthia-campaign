# Neridia's Gem
Given to the party by [[Lady Neridia]] in [[Arc 4 - Into the Elysian Oasis]]. She said it would help bring [[Xaphi]] back. In truth it was designed to shut the [[Aqua Font]] entirely and trigger a noble war. [[Helena]] was to throw it into the font after the water dance.
