# Walkie Talkies
The party's walkie talkies. [[Rael]] pocketed one during the phoenix explosion in [[Arc 5 - The Arid Expanse and Tajir]] and has been listening ever since. He used it to warn the party away from frozen Serenthia and, after taking [[William (Charlie)|Charlie]], to ask for a meeting.
