## Backstory
Very old, extremely forgetful wizard. Originally from the fey realm: leaving the fey realm makes you begin to forget things, which explains his condition. He does not remember his origins. He loves to drink and was a regular at [[Xaphi]]'s tavern. Extremely loyal to his friends and will do anything to protect them. His D&D Beyond sheet names him Aluinius Harm.

**Key trait:** things Aluinius makes up or misremembers have a habit of becoming real. He invented the town of [[Tinglewood]] and a daughter named [[Tinglewoodyallen]], and both turned out to be real. This reality-warping seems tied to his fey nature and is escalating.

## Hooks and goals
- [[Aluinius's True Identity]]: who is he within the fey court hierarchy?
- The goddess [[Cliodhna]] called out to him specifically at the [[Aqua Font]] ceremony.
- The unseelie [[Hollow Court]] has a standing order to detain him, found in [[Aelarion Frostbound]]'s war room. Aelarion's Herald, a Frost Shade, recognized him on sight. The unseelie want him detained, not harmed: they fear what he can do near fey portals.
- [[Tinglewood Is Real]]: his reality-warping needs to become undeniable to the party.
