## How to refresh a character
Ask Claude to refresh the party: it reads the sheets straight from D&D Beyond. If that ever fails, export the sheet as a PDF from D&D Beyond and drop it in `Inbox/`. Claude then updates the matching character note (level, stats, features, gear) and notes what changed.
