## Backstory
Born into the royal ruling family of [[Serenthia]], but swapped at birth by an evil midwife for the midwife's own child. The midwife raised Helena in [[Droughtfall]]. On her deathbed the midwife partially revealed the truth. Helena spent her life as a mercenary in the [[Submerged ring|Submerged Ring]], learning everything she could about the [[Elysian Oasis]] so she could infiltrate it and find her true parents.

[[Lady Neridia]] knew the truth about Helena's identity and used her deliberately, bringing her to the fertility festival as a pawn to destabilize [[House Aquadrion|House Aquadrian]].

## Hooks and goals
- [[Helena's Parents]]: her biological parents were in Serenthia when it flooded. Their fate is unknown, and one or both may survive inside the frozen ruins.
- The [[Tide Blade]] from the [[House Torentia]] armory bears a name on the pommel that Helena may recognize as a family connection.
- The false heir, the midwife's child who took her place, is encased in ice in a locked room in frozen Serenthia ([[The False Heir]]).
- The Criminal background on her sheet fits her mercenary years in the Submerged Ring.
