## Backstory
Autistic grung druid from a well-off grung family in the [[Submerged ring|Submerged Ring]]. Moved to [[Droughtfall]] to study the fungi and mold found there. His obsession with mushrooms and fungi has become narratively significant: mycelium networks in the fey realm appear to recognize him and work as communication lines between the material plane and the fey realm.

## Hooks and goals
- [[Xaphi in the Fey Realm]]: Jornt is the party's key to finding [[Xaphi]]. Fairy circles and mycelium nodes are telephone lines to the fey realm, and he is the only one who can use them. Protecting these nodes is now tactically critical.
- In [[Aelarion Frostbound]]'s war room, a fungal growth gave him a fragment of contact: a voice said his name. This was almost certainly Xaphi.
- The same mycelium patterns appeared in the ice shards left behind by dying Frost Shades.
- Water Dependency (from his sheet) in a desert world: the new ocean is a lifeline. Idea only, not canon.
