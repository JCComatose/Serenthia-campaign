## Backstory
Ran away from home after his blacksmith father refused to let him become a baker. Ended up in [[Droughtfall]] working at [[Xaphi]]'s tavern. His mother passed away when he was young. (Cook's utensils on his sheet fit the baker dream.)

## Hooks and goals
- His father survived the flood. He was working as a blacksmith in the [[Elysian Oasis]] castle and left a journal saying he is going out into the world to find Scott, believing his son is alive. He is walking toward [[Tajir]] and does not know [[William (Charlie)|Charlie]] exists. The arc around this is being planned in the "Plan outpost and volcano arc" thread.
- Scott acts as a father figure to [[William (Charlie)|Charlie]], which mirrors his own complicated relationship with his father. Charlie is now with [[Rael]].
