# Aelarion Frostbound
Commander of the [[Unseelie Hunt]]. A great hunter from unseelie mythos, not a fey himself. His forces hold frozen [[Serenthia]] and the [[Aqua Font]].

- On a timeline to secure the remaining [[Fey Portals]]. The volcano portal in the far north is his most active target.
- The Hunt pursues specific individuals. [[Aluinius]] is a target, under a [[Hollow Court]] order to detain him.
- His Herald, a Frost Shade, recognized Aluinius on sight.
- His war room under frozen Serenthia held [[Aelarion's Portal Map]], a requisition for a resupply outpost two days north, the Hollow Court letter, and a fungal growth linked to [[Xaphi]].
- Defeated the phoenix (jesse, 2026-10-12).
- Combat: CR 17 (bow and ice blade, ice-arrow volleys, legendary actions); full statblock in [[The Station and the Volcano]]. He can wipe the party if he wants to (jesse, 2026-10-12), but at the volcano he wants them alive for his trap.
- Now in [[The Fey Realm]], preparing to close the volcano portal from the other side if his Hunt can't convert it on this side. His captain leads the assault on the volcano. He wrote and signed the battle plans the party can find in the main tent at [[Hollis's Gas Station]] ([[The Station and the Volcano]]).

> [!secret] DM only: the end of the volcano session
> He comes out onto the summit at the end of [[The Station and the Volcano]], through the Hunt's converted portal or out of the one he closes. He dislikes the party for all the trouble they've caused him, but he plays friendly: he calls off his captain and points them to the sixth portal, the black circle on his map, farther south. It's a trap to get them into [[The Fey Realm]] (jesse, 2026-10-12). He is far above the party's level.
