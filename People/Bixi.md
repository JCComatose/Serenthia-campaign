# Bixi
A giant dragon turtle with a small forest on his back, home to [[Nergal]]'s cottage. He once floated in the [[Elysian Sea]] in the [[Elysian Oasis]]; since [[The Drowning]] he drifts on the [[New Ocean of Namaris]]. He carried the party out of the flood in [[Arc 5 - The Arid Expanse and Tajir]].
