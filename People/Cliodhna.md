# Cliodhna
A goddess of the [[Unseelie]] court, also described as a banshee queen who can talk to people in their sleep. She leads the unseelie charge in the mortal realm to reclaim the fey portals.

- She is at her most powerful during Serenthia's fertility festival, when the ruling heir dances to gain power over the [[Aqua Font]].
- At the ceremony in [[Arc 4 - Into the Elysian Oasis]] she called out to [[Aluinius]] specifically. He thought it was [[Xaphi]] and jumped into the font, bursting it open.
- Her song is tied to the prophetic dreams of [[Durnak the Drained]]'s "Drowned Draught".

> [!secret] DM only
> Through the burst Aqua Font, the unseelie now have a foothold. Her side's goal is to take the remaining [[Fey Portals]], which would make the world cold and wet. See [[Cliodhna's Endgame]].
