# Hollis
An old man who has kept a ruined old-world gas station in the [[Arid Expanse]] for as long as anyone remembers, and isn't leaving.

## Look, voice, mannerism
Sits on the porch in a rocking chair and doesn't look up. Answers in a few dry words, if at all.

## Wants and fears
To be left alone in his chair. He collects old-world tech and lights up for it.

## What they know
- The [[Unseelie Hunt]] set up its staging camp on his forecourt and uses his garage to repair their skiffs. He ignores them and they ignore him.
- How many of the Hunt are left at the station and where they are. He tells anyone who sits and asks, but never volunteers it.
- He claims the safe's code is his birthday, and that he forgot his birthday.

## Relationship to the party
Met in [[The Station and the Volcano]]. Not an enemy.

## Buys old-world tech
He buys any old-world tech the party brings him, such as radios and TVs, for 50 gp each, any time they come back with some.

> [!secret] DM only
> His line: "Everybody's fighting over the doors. Nobody ever thinks to shut 'em." A seed for [[Closing the Portals]]. Whether he knows more than he says is not decided yet.
