# Lady Neridia
Matriarch of [[House Neridia]], the house of commerce in [[Serenthia]].

- She identified [[Helena]] as the true heir and secretly used her to destabilize [[House Aquadrion|House Aquadrian]], hoping the power vacuum would let Neridia seize control.
- She gave the party [[Neridia's Gem]] under false pretenses, claiming it would help retrieve [[Xaphi]]. It was really designed to shut the [[Aqua Font]] entirely and trigger a noble war.
- Earlier, she worked with [[Nergal]] to overthrow House Aquadrian. Nergal joined her out of fear of the [[Unseelie]].
- Her scheme failed catastrophically. Whereabouts and survival unknown; possibly in the Serenthia ruins.
