# Maret
The mortal overseer of the [[Unseelie Hunt]]'s staging camp at [[Hollis's Gas Station]]. A logistics administrator, not a believer.

## Look, voice, mannerism
Not decided yet.

## Wants and fears
To survive. She will trade information for her life.

## What they know
- Works in the station's back office next to the safe, and knows the safe's combination. The safe is a portal to a flooded [[Fantasy Mart]].
- The Hunt's plan for the volcano, which is laid out in the main tent: the crater map, the note that water kills the sun fire, and the order to hit the summit at dusk.

## Relationship to the party
Met in [[The Station and the Volcano]].
