# Nergal
A retired blind grung water mage who once helped control the flow of the [[Aqua Font]] for the city (he was head water mage). He lives in a cottage on the back of a giant dragon turtle, [[Bixi]], who once floated in the [[Elysian Sea]] and now drifts on the [[New Ocean of Namaris]].

- Worked with [[Lady Neridia]] to overthrow [[House Aquadrion|House Aquadrian]], out of fear of the [[Unseelie]].
- [[Arc 4 - Into the Elysian Oasis]]: explained the fertility festival and the heir's water dance.
- [[Arc 5 - The Arid Expanse and Tajir]]: rescued the party after the flood, explained the [[Fey Portals|portal war]], and dropped them at [[Tajir]].

## Fear of the Unseelie
While head water mage, his curiosity got the best of him and he plunged into the waters of the Aqua Font. The power was too much: he saw visions all around him. To stop himself losing his mind, he made himself blind.
