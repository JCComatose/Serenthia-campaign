# Rael
A sand rider who founded the orphanage the party destroyed in [[Arc 5 - The Arid Expanse and Tajir]], and the de facto guardian of [[William (Charlie)|William]]. The lone surviving rider; he witnessed the poisoning and vowed revenge.

- Pocketed one of the party's [[Walkie Talkies|walkie talkies]] during the phoenix explosion and has been listening ever since.
- Has [[Aelarion's Portal Map|Aelarion's map of the expanse]] and knows about the portals.
- Warned the party over the walkie talkie that frozen Serenthia was dangerous (they don't know it was him).
- Took William from the ice sheet. William went willingly.
- Wants to meet the party at a waystation three hours northwest of Serenthia, at midday tomorrow.

> [!secret] DM only
> Rael is not a villain. He is a grieving man protecting the last child he has, and the most morally complicated person the party will meet. The discomfort is the point.
