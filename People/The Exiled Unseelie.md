# The Exiled Unseelie
A member of the unseelie court, exiled by his own family, found in the attic of [[The Fey Asylum]] in [[Arc 3 - The Fey Asylum]]. He wants the unseelie to be pure fey rather than open to outsiders. The party refused to help him. Name not recorded.
