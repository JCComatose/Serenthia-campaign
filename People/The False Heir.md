# The False Heir
The midwife's child, swapped at birth with [[Helena]] and raised as heir of the ruling house, [[House Aquadrion|House Aquadrian]]. Had been failing the heir's water dance at the fertility festival year after year.

- Found encased in ice in a locked room in frozen [[Serenthia]] ([[Arc 6 - Frozen Serenthia]]). Alive or dead is not recorded.
