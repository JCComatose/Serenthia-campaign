# The Rookie
The [[Aqua Wardens]] rookie assigned to guide the party in [[Arc 1 - Droughtfall and the Water Crisis]]. Secretly a double agent working to free [[Droughtfall]]'s lower class. He tried to recruit the party; they turned him in.

- Escaped. He scratched a message into his bunk in the frozen ruins: "I got out. The cause isn't dead. Look for us in the expanse."
- See [[The Droughtfall Movement]].
