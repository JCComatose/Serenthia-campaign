# Tinglewoodyallen
A daughter [[Aluinius]] made up, who turned out to be real. From [[Tinglewood]].

- The party brought her and her fire elemental lover back to [[Tajir]] ([[Arc 5 - The Arid Expanse and Tajir]]).
- When the elemental supercharged the phoenix egg, the ground turned to obsidian. She is presumed dead. Her baby may be sealed in [[The Obsidian Formation]].
