# William (Charlie)
The only child to survive when the party poisoned the orphanage's water ([[Arc 5 - The Arid Expanse and Tajir]]). Original name William; the party renamed him Charlie. [[Scott]] has been a father figure to him.

- Conflicted: [[Rael]] raised him, and the party killed his family.
- Left on the ice sheet above Serenthia in [[Arc 6 - Frozen Serenthia]]. Rael took him, and he went willingly.
- Scott's father does not know Charlie exists.
