# Xaphi
Runs the tavern in [[Droughtfall]] where [[Scott]] worked and [[Aluinius]] drank. She also secretly smuggled people between the rings of [[Serenthia]].

- Trapped in the fey realm: she did not return with the party from [[The Fey Asylum]] ([[Arc 3 - The Fey Asylum]]).
- Alive and trying to reach [[Jornt]] through mycelium networks. A fungal growth in [[Aelarion Frostbound]]'s war room said Jornt's name.
- Rescuing her is a long-term party goal. See [[Xaphi in the Fey Realm]].
