# Aluinius's True Identity
**Dramatic question:** Who is [[Aluinius]] within the fey courts, and why do the unseelie fear him?

## What is canon
- He came from the fey realm and lost his memory by leaving it.
- [[Cliodhna]] called out to him specifically at the Aqua Font ceremony.
- The [[Hollow Court]] has a standing order to detain him, not harm him: they fear what he can do near fey portals.
- [[Aelarion Frostbound]]'s Herald, a Frost Shade, recognized him on sight.
- His reality-warping is escalating ([[Tinglewood Is Real]]).

## Not decided yet
- His true identity and rank in the fey hierarchy.
