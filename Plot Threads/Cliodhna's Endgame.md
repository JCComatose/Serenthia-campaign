# Cliodhna's Endgame
> [!secret] DM only: this whole note

**Dramatic question:** Can the party stop the unseelie from taking the remaining [[Fey Portals]] before [[Namaris]] drowns and freezes?

## What is canon
1. **Done:** [[Cliodhna]] called [[Aluinius]] into the [[Aqua Font]], bursting open the unseelie portal and freezing [[Serenthia]].
2. **Now:** [[Aelarion Frostbound]] and the [[Unseelie Hunt]] hold the font and are racing to secure the other portals. The volcano is the most active target. The [[Hollow Court]] wants Aluinius detained.
3. **If they win:** the world turns cold and wet, then drowns and freezes over completely.

The race for the volcano is being planned in the "Plan outpost and volcano arc" thread.
