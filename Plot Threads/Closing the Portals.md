# Closing the Portals
> [!secret] DM only: this whole note
> The campaign's end goal, set by jesse on 2026-10-10. The campaign is about halfway through, so build toward this slowly.

**Dramatic question:** Can the party close every [[Fey Portals|fey portal]] and cut [[Namaris]] free of the war between the [[Seelie]] and [[Unseelie]]?

## What is canon
- From the material plane, a portal can only be converted between seelie and unseelie control. That is the race [[Aelarion Frostbound]] is running.
- From inside [[The Fey Realm]], a portal can be closed completely, one at a time.
- If every portal is closed, the seelie/unseelie war can no longer reach the material plane.

## What the party knows
- Nothing yet. They know about the portal war and the six portals ([[Aelarion's Portal Map]]), not that portals can be closed.

## Not decided yet
- How the party learns that portals can be closed: first hint planned for [[The Station and the Volcano]] (2026-10-12). A letter by [[Aelarion Frostbound]] in the Hunt's main tent says he has gone into the fey realm and will close the volcano portal completely if the Hunt can't convert it. Also [[Hollis]]'s line: "Nobody ever thinks to shut 'em." If the sun clan's ritual completes, the party watches Aelarion close the volcano portal for real.
- How they get into the fey realm on purpose. Planned (jesse, 2026-10-12): [[Aelarion Frostbound]] lures them to the sixth portal, the black circle farther south, as a trap. That is a later session; what waits on the other side is not decided yet.
- How either court reacts once it realizes the party wants to shut both of them out.
- What the world's climate becomes once no court controls it.
