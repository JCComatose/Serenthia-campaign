# Helena's Parents
**Dramatic question:** Are [[Helena]]'s royal parents alive in frozen [[Serenthia]], and what will she do if they are?

## What is canon
- They were in Serenthia when it flooded. One or both may survive inside the frozen ruins.
- The [[Tide Blade]] from the [[House Torentia]] armory bears a name she may recognize.
- [[The False Heir]] who took her place is encased in ice in the castle.
