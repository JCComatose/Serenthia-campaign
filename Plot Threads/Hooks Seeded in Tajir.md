# Hooks Seeded in Tajir
Plot hooks already written into the wares of [[Tajir]]'s merchants. Tick one off when the party picks it up.

- [ ] **The Vault of Avariel.** [[Aven Quicktide]]'s forged map leads to a real ruin of winged elves under the basin cliffs, infested with Fey-corrupted harpies.
- [ ] **The Eye Below.** A page torn from Aven's _Namaris Tide Atlas, Vol. III_ shows a route to a whirlpool at the heart of the [[Elysian Sea]].
- [ ] **The moving drowned city.** Aven's _Tidecaller's Sextant_ glimpses a drowned city beneath the waves under a full moon, in a new place each month.
- [ ] **The Drowned Draught.** [[Durnak the Drained]]'s black bottle gives a prophetic dream tied to [[Cliodhna]]'s song, possibly opening a rift to the Feywild.
- [ ] **Shalra's Blood Note.** One favor from [[Shalra “Coin-Tongue” Ven]], at the price of her burn scars and Fey hostility.
- [ ] **Irra's Ledger of the Lost.** [[Irra and the Coinwrights’ Bazaar|Irra]] scries on vanished merchants, and some say she writes the names of the dead to see if they still answer.
- [ ] **Shell of Recollection.** [[Luro the Shellwright]]'s nautilus reveals hidden Fey influences or portals near the Elysian Sea.
