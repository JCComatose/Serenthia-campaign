# Rael and William
**Dramatic question:** Will the party meet [[Rael]], and can there be any peace with the man whose family they killed?

## What is canon
- Rael has taken [[William (Charlie)|William]], who went willingly, and wants to meet at a waystation three hours northwest of Serenthia at midday tomorrow.
- He has detailed expanse maps and portal knowledge the party needs.
- [[Scott]]'s bond with Charlie is at stake. Missing the meeting may close the door on a willing alliance.

## What the party decided
- They chose the volcano over the meeting and told Rael over the walkie talkie to take care of Charlie. Charlie stays with Rael for now.

> [!secret] DM only
> Rael is not a villain. Don't steer the party: put the clock on the table and let them argue.
