# The Fallen Houses
**Dramatic question:** With [[Serenthia]] frozen, who among its noble houses survived, and what will they do next?

## What is canon
- [[House Aquadrion|House Aquadrian]] ruled; [[House Neridia]], [[House Torentia]] and [[House Celestia]] served under it.
- [[Lady Neridia]]'s scheme to seize power failed catastrophically. Her survival and next move are unknown.
- [[The False Heir]] is frozen in the castle. [[Helena]], the true heir, is with the party.
- Survivors and the [[Aqua Wardens]] are regrouping at [[Tajir]].
