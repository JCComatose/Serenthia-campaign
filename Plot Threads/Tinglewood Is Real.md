# Tinglewood Is Real
**Dramatic question:** How far does [[Aluinius]]'s reality-warping go, and when will the party have to admit it?

## What is canon
- Things Aluinius makes up or misremembers become real. He invented [[Tinglewood]] and [[Tinglewoodyallen]]; both exist.
- It seems tied to his fey nature, and it is escalating.

> [!secret] DM only
> The next time he invents something, it should be real in a way that cannot be dismissed. See also [[Aluinius's True Identity]].
