# Xaphi in the Fey Realm
**Dramatic question:** Can the party bring [[Xaphi]] home from [[The Fey Realm]]?

## What is canon
- She did not return from [[The Fey Asylum]]. She is alive and trying to reach [[Jornt]] through mycelium networks.
- Fairy circles and mycelium nodes are telephone lines to the fey realm, and Jornt is the only one who can use them. The node in [[The Abandoned Church]] is now inaccessible.
- A fungal growth in [[Aelarion Frostbound]]'s war room said Jornt's name.

> [!secret] DM only
> Every node they find is a phone line to Xaphi. Destroying a node closes it for good.
