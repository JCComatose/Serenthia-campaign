# The Arid Expanse
The vast desert covering most of [[Namaris]]: a hostile wilderness of roving gangs, sand raiders and desert monsters.

- Home of sand riders like [[Rael]] and the orphanage the party destroyed.
- [[The Droughtfall Movement]] is operating somewhere out here.
- Portals: the phoenix portal, the hot spring in the Eastern Expanse, the fire elemental in the deep desert ([[Fey Portals]]).
