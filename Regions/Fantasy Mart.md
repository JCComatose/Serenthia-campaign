# Fantasy Mart
_"Where all your dreams come true. Got a deal for you."_ A magical market the party has shopped at before.

## Branches
- **The flooded branch:** reached through the safe in [[Maret]]'s back office at [[Hollis's Gas Station]] ([[The Station and the Volcano]]). A knee-high tide line on every shelf, buckled linoleum, ink-bled price tags and a flickering light. Music: `Music/Fantasy_Mart_safe.mp3`.
