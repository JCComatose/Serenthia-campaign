# Hollis's Gas Station
A ruined old-world gas station in the desert north of [[Serenthia]], where the [[Unseelie Hunt]] staged its attack on the volcano.

## First impression
A rusted sign rises out of the heat haze long before anything else. Pumps half buried in sand, pale tents and stacked crates on the forecourt, tools clanking in the garage, and an old man rocking on the porch who doesn't look up.

## Notable places
- **The porch:** [[Hollis]]'s rocking chair.
- **The garage:** two Hunt mechanics repair a skiff the party can take. An old flare gun sits on the workbench.
- **The mart (Fantasy Mart):** dusty shelves, dead coolers, the register (450 gp and Hunt water chits) and a chest freezer holding a dead sun clan member and their ritual robes. The PA system still works and plays a crackly soda special ([[Intercom soda announcement.mp3]]). A door in the side wall opens into the garage.
- **The back office:** [[Maret]]'s desk and the safe, which is really a portal to a flooded [[Fantasy Mart]].
- **The main tent** (out back, frost hound chained outside): the Hunt's full plan for the volcano, including the letter about their agent in the fey realm.
- **The forecourt:** crates and the water pump where [[Pip]] works. The tents are out back.

## Who is here
Hollis, Maret and Pip, two Hunt mechanics, two Hunt soldiers on watch and a chained frost hound. Most of the Hunt has already left for the volcano.

## Trouble brewing
The Hunt plans to hit the volcano's summit at dusk, while a sun-worshipping tribe performs a ritual in the crater. See [[The Station and the Volcano]].
