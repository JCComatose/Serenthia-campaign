# New Ocean of Namaris
The ocean formed by the flooding from the [[Aqua Font]] when [[Aluinius]] jumped in ([[The Drowning]]). It is still growing as the waters of [[Serenthia]] flow out across [[The Great Basin]] and the old riverbeds. Not the same as the [[Elysian Sea]].

- [[Tajir]] is the last solid land before it.
- [[Bixi]] the dragon turtle drifts on it, carrying [[Nergal]].
- Drowned Serenthia lies beneath it; Tajir's merchants dive and salvage its ruins.
