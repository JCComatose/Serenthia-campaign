# The Great Basin
The basin where the elves found the oasis that became [[Serenthia]]. [[Tajir]] sits on the sun-bleached cliffs along its northern edge.

- Since [[The Drowning]] the basin is flooded, with frozen Serenthia in it, becoming the [[New Ocean of Namaris]].
- Rumor: [[Aven Quicktide]] sells a "partially real" map to the Vault of Avariel, a ruin of winged elves under the basin cliffs, now infested with fey-corrupted harpies.
