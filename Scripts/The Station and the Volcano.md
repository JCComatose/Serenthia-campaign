# The Station and the Volcano
%% Draft, built scene by scene with jesse. Scene 4 (the road to the volcano and the tribe's camp) is still being planned.

The party is level 7: [[Helena]], [[Jornt]], [[Aluinius]] and [[Scott]], plus a guest player's hitchhiker for this one session. They left [[William (Charlie)|Charlie]] with [[Rael]] and chose the volcano. No clock this session: the crew makes it in time.

## Off the ice
_Dramatic question: what do they leave behind on the ice?_

> [!read-aloud] The ice sheet
> [[Ice wind loop.mp3|Ice wind loop]] The wind across the ice sheet has teeth. Thirty feet below your boots, the spires of [[Serenthia]] stand frozen in the act of drowning. Your sand skiff waits where you left it, sail snapping, runners crusted white. Charlie's small footprints lead away from it and stop where hoofprints begin. Far to the north, past the edge of the ice, a thin black column of smoke rises from the horizon like a crack in the sky.

- **The footprints** show Charlie went willingly: no drag marks, and he climbed up onto the mount himself. Anyone who looks sees it. No roll.
- **The smoke** is the volcano. It gets a little thicker every time someone looks north.
- **The skiff on ice** flies, faster than on sand. Let whoever pilots it enjoy that.

## The gas station
_Dramatic question: can they get the Hunt's plans without the whole camp coming down on them?_

> [!read-aloud] The station
> [[Garage work loop.mp3|Garage work loop]] A rusted sign rises out of the heat haze long before anything else: a faded old-world gas station, its pumps half buried in sand. Stacked crates crowd its forecourt, pale tents poke up from a junkyard of rusted wrecks behind it, and from inside the garage comes the clank of tools on metal. On the porch an old man rocks slowly in his chair. He doesn't look up.

![[gas_station.png]]
Show this as they pull up.

This is the [[Unseelie Hunt]]'s staging camp for the attack on the volcano, set up in front of [[Hollis's Gas Station]]. Most of the Hunt has already left to prepare the assault, so the camp is thin.

### Who is here
- **[[Hollis]]** rocks on the porch. He ignores everyone, the Hunt included, and they ignore him. He never volunteers anything. If someone sits down and talks to him, he answers in a few dry words: how many are left, and where they are.
  - **He collects old-world tech.** The one thing that makes him look up: show him an old radio, TV or similar and he'll buy it for 50 gp each, now or any time they find more.
- **On watch:** two [[#Hunt Soldier|Hunt soldiers]], one on the station roof with a longbow and one walking among the crates.
- **In the garage:** two [[#Hunt Mechanic|Hunt mechanics]], busy working on a skiff.
- **Out back in the junkyard:** a frost hound, chained near the main tent.
- **[[Maret]]**, the mortal overseer, works in the back office with the safe. She is not a believer and will trade information to stay alive.
- **[[Pip]]**, seventeen, pumps water on the forecourt. He doesn't really know who he works for and will talk to anyone who shares food.

That's five fighters at most, so sneaking and fighting both work. Let the players pick.

If all five fight at once it's a medium fight for five level 7s. If the party picks them off one at a time it's easy. Either way they should reach the volcano with most of their spells and hit points, because the crater is the big fight.

**Morale:** the mechanics surrender as soon as one of them drops or both soldiers fall. A soldier who is bloodied (half HP or less) tries to run. Nobody here wants to die for a camp the Hunt already left.

> [!secret] Hollis's line
> If the party talks with him long enough, or mentions the portals: "Everybody's fighting over the doors. Nobody ever thinks to shut 'em." A seed for [[Closing the Portals]]. Don't explain it.

### The main tent
The biggest tent in the middle of the junkyard, with the frost hound chained outside. The Hunt's whole plan for the volcano is laid out on a camp table, every page written and signed by [[Aelarion Frostbound]] himself:
- **The Hunt's force:** 12 Hunt riders and 4 frost hounds under a captain, riding for the summit at dusk, when the sun creatures weaken.
- **The sun clan:** the scouts count about 30 at their camp on a ridge just below the summit, and 4 warriors guarding the priest at the rim, 40 feet above the camp.
- **The ritual:** a scout's sketch of the crater shows three braziers of sun glass around a kneeling priest inside a dome of golden light. If the chanting finishes, the mountain erupts and the volcano portal goes to the [[Seelie]].
- **How to stop it:** "Water kills the sun fire. Frost is too slow." Put out or break all three braziers and the dome drops. Then break the priest's trance: hurt him, silence him, or drag him out of his circle.
- **The last resort:** a letter under the orders explains that Aelarion has gone into [[The Fey Realm]] to fight on the other side of the portal. If the Hunt can't convert the portal on this side, he will close it completely from there. He admits a closed portal will cost the [[Unseelie]] power if they win the war, but it is better than leaving it to the Seelie.
- Also in the tent: a Hunt hunting horn (blow it and frost hounds hesitate for 1 round, thinking you're pack) and 2 Potions of Fire Resistance brewed from [[Cinderroot]].

The party has Vials of Living Water from the [[House Torentia]] armory. The note is there so they think of them. Don't make them roll to understand it.

> [!secret] Why the letter matters
> This is the first time the party learns a portal can be closed from the fey realm side. It is the seed for [[Closing the Portals]]. Aelarion is not at the volcano; the Hunt's captain leads the assault. He already beat the phoenix, so his confidence in the letter is earned.

## The road to the volcano
_Dramatic question: can they outrun the thing under the sand?_

The party is racing for the volcano, so this is a quick chase, not a full fight. Six rounds, then they reach the lower slope and safety.

> [!read-aloud] Something under the sand
> The station shrinks behind you and the volcano fills the sky ahead. Then the pilot feels it before anyone sees it: a shudder through the runners, like the skiff just rolled over a drum. Behind you, the sand begins to ripple. A long line of humps races along under the dunes, gaining. The desert bursts open. A centipede the length of a river barge rears up out of the sand, hundreds of legs clattering like hail on a tin roof, its mandibles dripping green acid that hisses where it lands. It hits the ground running, straight at you.

[[Centipede burst.mp3|Centipede burst]] Play this as the desert bursts open.

[[Desert_pursuit.mp3|Desert Pursuit: the chase]] Play this the moment it bursts out of the sand, with loop switched on in the Sound panel. It crossfades out whatever music is playing.

### Running the chase
![[battlemap_desert_chase.png]]
Battle map for the chase.

**The track:** draw a strip 10 squares long with three lanes: left, center and right. The skiff sits at the front in the center lane. The [[#Dune Centipede|dune centipede]] starts 6 squares behind. If they last 6 rounds, they hit the black glass of the lower slope, and the centipede won't follow onto the hot rock.

**Each round:**
1. **Telegraph.** Read that round's hazard from the list below, and roll a d3 for the lane the centipede rears toward (1 left, 2 center, 3 right). Describe both out loud: "It coils to the left, acid bubbling in its jaws. Dead ahead, a rock spire rises out of the sand."
2. **The pilot dodges.** Shifting one lane is free. Jumping two lanes takes a DC 13 Dexterity check (add proficiency with vehicles). On a failure the skiff only shifts one lane.
3. **The crew slows it.** Everyone else takes one action. Each of these earns 1 slow:
   - Landing a hit or spell for 10 or more damage.
   - Dumping cargo in its path (DC 12 Strength).
   - Spells that make difficult terrain (Jornt's Spike Growth, Plant Growth and the like).
   - Distracting it (Cornelia): a DC 14 Performance or Deception check also moves its telegraphed lane one to the left or right.
   - The flare gun in its face counts as 2.
4. **The centipede strikes the telegraphed lane.** From 4 or more squares back, it spits acid. From 1 to 3 squares back, it lunges with a bite. If the skiff isn't in that lane, the attack misses and splashes harmlessly in the sand.
5. **It closes in.** It moves 2 squares closer, minus the slows: with 1 slow it moves 1, with 2 it holds, and with 3 or more it drops back 1.

**At 0 squares** it latches onto the stern. It bites every round with no telegraph and no dodging until someone deals 20 damage to it in one round or makes a DC 15 Strength check to pry it off, which knocks it back to 2 squares.

If nobody slows it, it catches them in round 3. If they kill it, the chase is over, but it costs them resources they want for the crater.

### Hazards, round by round
The pilot has to dodge each hazard. If the centipede strikes the hazard's lane, it plows into the hazard too.

| Round | Hazard | Lane | If the skiff hits it | If the centipede strikes it |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | A rock spire juts from the sand. | Center | 2d10 bludgeoning to everyone aboard; DC 13 Dex save or thrown prone on the deck. | It slams into the spire headfirst: counts as 2 slows. |
| 2 | A buried old-world bus, half sunk in a dune. | Left | Runners tangle: the pilot has disadvantage on next round's check. | It tears the bus apart and keeps coming. Shrapnel: everyone aboard makes a DC 12 Dex save or takes 2d6 slashing. |
| 3 | A sand geyser, steam bursting from a vent. | Right | 3d6 fire to everyone aboard, DC 13 Con save for half. | Scalded: it takes 3d6 fire and counts as 1 slow. |
| 4 | A crevasse splits the desert. | Left and center | The pilot must jump it: DC 15 Dex. On a failure the skiff lurches, 2d10 bludgeoning to everyone aboard, and the centipede gains 1 extra square. | It bridges the gap without slowing. |
| 5 | An ash flurry rolls in from the mountain. | All lanes | You don't announce the telegraph this round. A DC 14 Perception check hears which lane it's coiling toward. | No effect. It hunts by vibration. |
| 6 | Lava bombs rain onto the slope ahead. | Right | 4d6 fire to everyone aboard, DC 14 Dex save for half. | It takes 4d6 fire and counts as 1 slow. |

After round 6, the skiff hits the black glass of the lower slope. If the centipede is still latched on, it lets go with a shriek as its legs touch the hot rock.

## The crater
_Dramatic question: can they break the priest's trance before the mountain wakes?_

A desert raider tribe that worships the sun and the [[Seelie]] court is performing a ritual to erupt the volcano and wake a volcanic tortoise (a fiery mirror of [[Bixi]]). There are no Seelie fey in the world; the tribe hears "the sun" in the heat haze. Round 1 is the first round of the fight on the ridge. ==If the priest is still chanting at the end of round 8, the ritual completes.== See [[#If the ritual completes]].

### Catching up with the Hunt
> [!read-aloud] The slope
> The skiff grinds up onto the black glass of the lower slope, and for the first time all day nothing is chasing you. Then you see them. Halfway up the mountain, a column of pale riders on frost-white mounts picks its way up the switchbacks, frost hounds loping at their heels, ice crusting on the rock wherever they pass. Far above them, at the very rim of the crater, golden light is gathering. Small robed figures are dragging three great braziers into place, and the first notes of a chant roll down the mountainside.

![[black_volcano.png]]
Show this as the volcano comes into view.

The [[Unseelie Hunt]] got here first and is still climbing. The sun clan holds the rim and is getting the ritual started. The party catches up with the back of the Hunt's column on the narrow trail, well below the rim.

**The decision.** The Hunt's captain turns in the saddle and sees them. He knows [[Aluinius]] on sight, since the eladrin is the Hunt's target, but he has a bigger problem above him. His offer: "Truce until the fire's out." Don't give the players a menu. Let them talk, scheme or draw steel, and follow what they do:
- **They accept:** they climb together. See [[#If they team up with the Hunt]].
- **They refuse, or slip past the column:** the party and the Hunt reach the rim at about the same time, and it's a three-way battle. See [[#If it's a three-way battle]].
- **They attack the Hunt here:** fight on the narrow switchbacks with the drop at their backs. The chanting starts above them, so the round 8 count starts now instead of on the ridge.

The hunting horn from the main tent, or [[Maret]] if she came along, gets the party a fair hearing with the captain.

When they reach the ridge:

> [!read-aloud] The ridge and the rim
> [[Volcano rumble loop.mp3|Volcano rumble loop]] The trail levels out onto a broad ridge just below the summit, and it's crowded: hide tents, cook fires and the sun clan, dozens of them, painted in gold ash. Their beasts pace between the tents, lions with manes of living flame and scorpions glowing like coals. Behind the camp, a wall of black rock rises forty feet to the crater's rim, hot enough that the air shimmers above it. Up there, a robed figure kneels with his arms raised to the sun, and the air around him bends inside a dome of golden light. Three great braziers of sun glass blaze around him, and every time the chanting rises, the whole mountain answers.

### The ridge camp and the cliff
[[Oh-Ey-Ah.mp3|Oh-Ey-Ah: crater battle]] Play this when the fight on the ridge starts, with loop switched on in the Sound panel. It crossfades out whatever music is playing.

![[battlemap_volcano_lower.png]]
Battle map for the ridge camp and the foot of the cliff.

The sun clan's camp (about 30 of them) sits on a ridge just below the summit. The ritual is 40 feet higher, at the rim, so this whole fight is uphill: through the camp, then up the cliff.
- **The ridge camp:** the clan's warriors and beasts fight here. Tents block line of sight. A creature shoved into a cook fire takes 1d10 fire.
- **The trail onto the ridge** is loose scree: difficult terrain.
- **The cliff to the rim** is 40 feet of black rock. Ways up:
  - **The stair:** narrow steps cut into the rock, single file. Two warriors hold the top.
  - **Climbing:** DC 13 Athletics, at half speed.
  - **Three rope ladders** the clan uses. Anyone at the top can cut one as an action, and whoever is on it falls.
  - **Magic:** flying, Misty Step, Spider Climb, or whatever Jornt's plants can do with a rock wall.
- **High ground:** anyone at the rim can drop a rock on a climber below as an action: DC 13 Dex save or 2d6 bludgeoning, and a climber who fails falls.
- **Falling** from the cliff: 4d6 bludgeoning, landing prone on the ridge.
- **The Hunt's ice ramp:** the Hunt's riders freeze a ramp of ice up the cliff. It's finished at the end of round 3, and anyone can use it after that, the party and the clan included.

### The braziers and the dome
![[battlemap_volcano_upper.png]]
Battle map for the rim, where the ritual is.

- Nothing passes through the dome while any brazier still burns.
- Each brazier: AC 15, 45 HP, immune to fire. A Vial of Living Water poured in deals 25 damage. Shape Water deals 2d10.
- Anyone who ends their turn next to a brazier takes a flare: DC 14 Dex save or 2d6 fire.
- When the last brazier breaks, the dome drops.

### The priest
The clan calls him [[#The Sunspeaker|the Sunspeaker]].
- Once the dome is down, any hit forces a Concentration save: DC 10 or half the damage, whichever is higher.
- A failed save, a Silence spell, or shoving him out of his circle **pauses the ritual**. The mountain sighs and the eye in the crater wall closes. See [[#If they stop the ritual]].
- He can't move while he chants, but he still fights from inside the dome with Searing Word and Sunglare.

### The defenders
Most of the 30 on the ridge are the clan's elders, children and beast handlers. They shout, throw stones and pull back into the tents. These are the ones who fight:
- **On the ridge (first wave):** 3 [[#Sun Clan Warrior|sun clan warriors]], 2 sun-maned lions, 1 glowing scorpion and 2 sun hawks.
- **At the rim (second wave):** [[#The Sunspeaker|the Sunspeaker]] inside the dome and 4 sun clan warriors. Two hold the top of the stair and two watch the ladders, dropping rocks and cutting ropes.

**How the Hunt changes it:**
- **If they team up:** the riders take the scorpion and one warrior on the ridge, and after round 3 two riders go up the ice ramp to hit a brazier. Describe it; don't roll it.
- **If it's a three-way battle:** use the attrition rule in [[#If it's a three-way battle]], and take what drops out of the waves above.
- **If they fought the Hunt on the slope:** both waves at full strength, and nobody is coming to help. This is the deadliest path.

**Dialing it at the table:**
- **Too easy:** at the start of a round, two more warriors come out of the tents.
- **Close to a wipe:** the clan doesn't finish off a downed enemy. Two warriors drag them toward the rim as an offering to the mountain, which takes 2 rounds, and they throw them into the crater at the end of the second. That gives the others two rounds to save them. If nobody does, that character dies, and this is how the fight can kill the whole party.
- **The lions break and run** the first time a tremor roll comes up 5 or 6.

The Hunt wants the ritual stopped too, so it goes for the braziers first. What happens next depends on what the party chose on the slope.

### If they team up with the Hunt
They took the captain's truce on the slope and crest the rim together, "until the fire's out." The riders go for the braziers and the sun clan, and leave the party alone. This makes the crater fight much easier. The cost comes after the ritual stops.

### If it's a three-way battle
The Hunt goes for the braziers first and [[Aluinius]] second. The sun clan attacks whoever is closest to the priest. Don't roll Hunt against clan: at the end of each round, one rider and one lion or warrior drop, and you describe it. The party can let the two sides wear each other down, but every round they wait is another tremor roll and a round closer to round 8.

### If they stop the ritual
The mountain sighs and the eye in the crater wall closes. Then the Hunt starts its own conversion of the portal. Frost is slow: riders hold the rim while frost creeps over the portal, and it takes 3 rounds.

> [!secret] The captain's deal
> With the fire out, the captain turns to the party: "Give us the eladrin and walk away." [[Aluinius]] is the Hunt's target. If they refuse, it's a fight against a Hunt that also has to hold the portal. The party can also break the conversion, and then neither court gets the portal.

Either way, [[#Aelarion on the summit|Aelarion arrives]] at the end:
- **If the Hunt converts the portal,** he steps through the new frost portal.
- **If the party breaks the conversion,** he does what his letter promised. [[Portal collapse.mp3|Portal collapse]] The portal goes black and collapses, and he steps out of it as it closes.

### If the ritual completes
> [!read-aloud] The mountain wakes
> [[Tortoise rising.mp3|Tortoise rising]] The chant ends on one note that you feel in your teeth. The portal flares gold, and the crater floor heaves upward. A shell the size of a hill rises out of the magma, crusted with black rock and glowing at every seam, and a head like a burning boulder swings up toward the rim. Then the gold light flickers. Frost races across the portal from the inside, and with a sound like a frozen lake cracking, the portal goes black and folds in on itself. It snaps shut halfway through the beast, and the tortoise screams.

[[Aelarion Frostbound]] closed the portal from the fey side, just as his letter promised. Only the front half of the [[#Half-risen Magma Tortoise|tortoise]] made it through. It is pinned in the crater wall, it can't move, and it's furious.

**Aelarion steps out** of the collapsing portal a moment later and fights the tortoise alongside everyone else:
- At initiative 0 each round, he deals 25 cold damage to the tortoise. Its lava crust freezes and cracks where he hits, so the next attack against it this round has advantage.
- He never takes the killing blow. If his damage would drop it, it stays at 1 HP, and he steps back: "Finish it."
- When the tortoise falls, the mountain goes quiet. With the portal closed, the eruption chokes off.

### Aelarion on the summit
All the endings meet here, with Aelarion standing on the rim and the Hunt around him.

> [!read-aloud] Aelarion Frostbound
> Frost spreads from his boots across the black rock, and the steam of the crater turns to snow around him. He is tall, pale and unhurried, wrapped in a cloak of white fur, with a hunter's bow across his back. He looks at each of you in turn, as if he's counting, and then he smiles. "So you're the ones who keep getting in my way."

[[Aelarion Frostbound]] doesn't like the party: they have caused him trouble since [[Serenthia]]. But he plays friendly. He calls off the captain ("Enough. Let them go."), thanks them for the fight, and points them south, to the black circle on [[Aelarion's Portal Map]]. It's his map, and he knows they took it. "You want this war to end? Go there."

> [!secret] Why he's friendly
> It's a trap. The black circle is the sixth portal, farther south, and it leads into [[The Fey Realm]]. Aelarion wants the party to walk through it. Going there is a later session.
> - What waits on the other side: not decided yet.
> - If they attack him, see below.

**If they attack him:** he can kill the whole party in two rounds, and the Hunt around him joins in. He would rather not, because he needs them alive to walk through his portal. He fights for real for one round, marking whoever struck first as his quarry. When the first character drops, he stops: "That is what I could have done. Go south." If they keep fighting after that, he stops holding back, and a total party kill is on the table. His legendary resistances also make him hard to pin with a spell, so a lucky Hold Person won't end this.
