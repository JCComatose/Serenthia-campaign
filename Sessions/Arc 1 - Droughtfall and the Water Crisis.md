# Arc 1: Droughtfall and the Water Crisis
_Source: campaign bible exported from jesse's planning chat, 2026-10-10. Session numbers within the arc are not recorded._

## What happened
- The party met in [[Droughtfall]] at an outdoor town meeting called over tighter water rationing.
- A mysterious figure on the wall between Droughtfall and the [[Submerged ring|Submerged Ring]] threw dust over the crowd and escaped unnoticed.
- A baby sand shark attacked, and the party stopped it.
- The [[Aqua Wardens]], the Submerged Ring's police, recruited them to find who was siphoning the city's water. The [[Aqua Warden Chief]] assigned a rookie to guide them ([[The Rookie]]).
- Sewers, markets, an underwater diving race, and a fight with a giant sea monster led them to the hideout of a major crime organization, whose boss they defeated.
- The rookie was a double agent trying to free Droughtfall's lower class. He tried to recruit the party; they declined and turned him in.

## Rewards
- Lifetime visas to travel freely within the Submerged Ring.

## Unresolved
- The man who threw the dust was never identified.
