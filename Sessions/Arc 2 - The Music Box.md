# Arc 2: The Music Box
_Source: campaign bible exported from jesse's planning chat, 2026-10-10. Session numbers within the arc are not recorded._

## What happened
- The party took a job to retrieve a stolen [[Music Boxes|music box]].
- Clues in the box's legend song led them to caverns, where a researcher had taken it to unlock its mysteries.
- They defeated an undead captain and found a jewel that fit the box.
- When opened, the box played its song and compelled [[Aluinius]] to walk to an [[The Abandoned Church|abandoned church]] in the Submerged Ring, run by a cult.
- Inside was a fairy circle of mushrooms. There was a flash, and the party disappeared.
