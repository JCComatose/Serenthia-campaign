# Arc 3: The Fey Asylum
_Source: campaign bible exported from jesse's planning chat, 2026-10-10. Session numbers within the arc are not recorded._

## What happened
- The party arrived in the fey realm inside an abandoned insane asylum sealed by magical barriers ([[The Fey Asylum]]).
- They met [[Arty Ramsden]], a Salvador Dalí-esque blood magic fey who finger-paints with severed fingers, and [[Booker]], an Italian mob boss gnome with invisible but real imaginary family members.
- In the attic they found [[The Exiled Unseelie]], a member of the unseelie court exiled by his own family, who wants the unseelie to be pure fey rather than open to outsiders.
- They refused to help him and were sent back to the material plane.
- Back home, the researcher had befriended the cult. The party realized [[Xaphi]] had not come back with them.
