# Arc 4: Into the Elysian Oasis
_Source: campaign bible exported from jesse's planning chat, 2026-10-10. Session numbers within the arc are not recorded._

## What happened
- Seeking a way to retrieve [[Xaphi]], the party learned the [[Elysian Oasis]] is a portal to the fey realm.
- They were directed to [[Helena]] at the Submerged Ring's bathhouse, where mercenary deals are made and where [[Chet]], the legendary dapper, works. Scott failed Chet's dap. Helena agreed to help.
- They infiltrated a noble fundraiser and were brought into the Elysian Oasis by nobles as escorts to the fertility festival. Weapons and trackers were confiscated. [[Aluinius]] told the guard his wand of wonder was an adult toy; later that day a guard accidentally cast fireball on himself with it.
- [[Lady Neridia]] knew Helena's true identity and wanted to use her to destabilize [[House Aquadrion|House Aquadrian]]. She brought them to [[Nergal]].
- Nergal explained that at the fertility festival the goddess [[Cliodhna]] is at her most powerful, and the ruling heir performs a water dance to gain power over the [[Aqua Font]]. The false heir ([[The False Heir]]) had been failing year after year.
- The plan: Helena performs the dance and throws [[Neridia's Gem]] into the font "to help bring Xaphi back." That was a lie: the gem would have closed the font entirely and created a power vacuum.
- At the ceremony Helena danced. Guards attacked. At the critical moment Cliodhna called out to Aluinius. He thought it was Xaphi calling and jumped into the Aqua Font.
- The font burst open. [[Serenthia]] flooded entirely, then froze. See [[The Drowning]].
