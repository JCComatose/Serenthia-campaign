# Arc 5: The Arid Expanse and Tajir
_Source: campaign bible exported from jesse's planning chat, 2026-10-10. Session numbers within the arc are not recorded._

## What happened
- [[Nergal]] rescued the party on his dragon turtle [[Bixi]] and explained the seelie/unseelie portal war ([[Fey Portals]]).
- He dropped them at a merchant outpost on the edge of the new ocean: [[Tajir]], which began growing into a town.
- The party helped Tajir by dealing with sand raiders to the north, who turned out to run an orphanage. The party poisoned their water supply, killing all but one child: William, whom the party renamed Charlie ([[William (Charlie)]]).
- A lone surviving sand rider, [[Rael]], witnessed this and vowed revenge. He pocketed one of the party's [[Walkie Talkies|walkie talkies]] during the phoenix explosion.
- The party intercepted a crew transporting a phoenix egg to the ocean (to kill the phoenix portal). They fought them on a sand train, detached the egg car, and buried the egg under their home in Tajir.
- They fetched wood from the real town of [[Tinglewood]] (which Aluinius had invented) and brought back [[Tinglewoodyallen]] (also real, also invented by Aluinius) and her fire elemental lover.
- They put the fire elemental in the basement where the egg was buried. The elemental supercharged the egg and the ground around their home turned to obsidian. Tinglewoodyallen is presumed dead. The egg, the elemental, and possibly her baby are sealed in the obsidian. See [[The Obsidian Formation]].
