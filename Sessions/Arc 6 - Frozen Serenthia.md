# Arc 6: Frozen Serenthia
_Source: campaign bible exported from jesse's planning chat, 2026-10-10. Session numbers within the arc are not recorded._

_This is the current arc._

## What happened
- The party returned to [[Serenthia]], now frozen solid. A mysterious voice on the [[Walkie Talkies|walkie talkie]] warned them it was dangerous inside. (DM: this was [[Rael]]; the party doesn't know yet.)
- They left [[William (Charlie)|Charlie]] on the ice sheet above and went down.
- In [[Aelarion Frostbound]]'s war room they found:
  - [[Aelarion's Portal Map]]: all six portal locations, two crossed out.
  - A requisition document for a resupply outpost two days north.
  - A letter from the [[Hollow Court]] with a standing order to detain [[Aluinius]].
  - A fungal growth through which [[Jornt]] got a fragment of contact: someone said his name (almost certainly [[Xaphi]]).
- They found the [[House Torentia]] armory: weapons, armor, Vials of Living Water, the [[Tide Blade]], and the Warden's Cloak.
- In a locked room they found [[The False Heir]] encased in ice.
- [[The Rookie]]'s message was scratched into his bunk in the ruins: "I got out. The cause isn't dead. Look for us in the expanse."
- Scott's father, a blacksmith in the [[Elysian Oasis]] castle, survived and left a journal there saying he is going out into the world to find Scott. _Whether the party has found the journal isn't recorded._
- When they came back up, Charlie was gone. Rael had taken him. Over the walkie talkie Rael said William is safe, that he has been listening since the phoenix, that he knows about the portals, and that he wants to meet at a waystation three hours northwest at midday tomorrow.

## Loot
- Weapons and armor from the Torentia armory, Vials of Living Water, the Tide Blade, the Warden's Cloak. _Who carries what isn't recorded._

## Where we stopped
The party is on the ice sheet above Serenthia. They must decide whether to meet Rael first or press north toward the outpost and the volcano portal.
